Ingredient award on total cost

Total cost = price + freight + reject penalty + carrying cost of lead time. Reject penalty = ppm / 1,000,000 × 25 × price. Carrying cost = 12% a year. The award is the eligible quote with the lowest total cost, not the lowest unit price.
