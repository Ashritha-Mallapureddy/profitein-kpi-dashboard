What a C-level review would decide
  1. No new outer zone until the next monsoon has run on the split route. June 2025 is the evidence, not a closed chapter.

How to read the quote chart. Each ingredient has its own three suppliers. Blue is the unit price on the quote. Orange is the extra: freight, a penalty for rejects, and the cost of waiting for the lead time. The white number is the unit price. The number after the orange tip is the full cost. Not eligible means under 93% on time, so the cheaper blue bar cannot win. Award is the eligible supplier with the lowest full cost.
